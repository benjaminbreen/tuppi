# Ašḫella image cutouts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then copied here as transparent PNGs. The brief for every asset was a single, complete object or action with space around all four sides, naturalistic material detail, no invented temple or crowd, and no text. These are interpretive reconstructions; the pictured breed, colour, tool form and arrangement are not manuscript evidence.
