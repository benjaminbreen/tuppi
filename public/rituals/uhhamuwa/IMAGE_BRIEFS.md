# Uḫḫamuwa image set

These 16 images were generated as separate transparent PNG cutouts with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are interpretive reconstructions, not evidence that a particular object looked exactly this way. The corresponding step dossiers distinguish what KUB 9.31 states from the choices made for an image.

## Shared visual brief

> One isolated physical subject or compact object group, meticulously rendered as a plausible Late Bronze Age Anatolian material reconstruction. Museum-quality, photorealistic detail, natural texture, restrained warm palette, soft directional light, editorial simplicity. Compose for legibility at full-page and thumbnail sizes. Genuine transparent alpha with clear corners. No full people, faces, architecture, landscape, text, decorative frame, fantasy objects or modern equipment. Do not add ritual equipment the text does not name.
